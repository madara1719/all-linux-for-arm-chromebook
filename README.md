# How to install 

Make sure your device is not managed by a school or third-party agent, first read the device support information, choose your preferred operating system, and follow the steps below. 

PD: Get another device, either a phone or another laptop to follow the instructions, or you can write it down in a notebook or somewhere if you only have your Chromebook.

## Step 1: Enable developer mode  
WARNING: This will erase all of your data, be sure of making a backup of your important data before doing this process.This may void your warranty too.
